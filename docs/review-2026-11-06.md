## Remaining findings

These pre-existing behavior issues were reproduced with mocked Foundry documents. They are not repaired by this stylesheet and convention change.

### High: a purchase can partially complete before a shop stock write fails

Location: `scripts/shop-class.js`, `applyPurchaseToBuyer` and the buy listener's call to `decrementShopStock`.

The buyer receives items and loses coins before the shop's embedded item is updated. If the latter rejects (for example, a player lacks ownership of a GM-owned limited-stock shop), the buyer changes remain and stock is unchanged. Concurrent buyers also calculate from independently read quantities. A follow-up should perform purchases through an authorized GM operation, serialize stock changes and define failure recovery for the multi-document writes. A second ownership check alone does not make the transaction consistent.

Reproduction: open a limited-stock shop, request two items at five coins each and make the shop stock update reject. The buyer item creation and coin debit to 90 from 100 have already completed.

### Medium: negative prices credit the buyer

Location: `scripts/shop-class.js`, buy listener price/total-cost calculation.

`Number(item.system.price) || 0` accepts negative values. A price of -5 for two items updates a buyer with 100 coins to 110. Require finite, non-negative prices before any document writes; invalid configuration should produce a visible notification.

### Medium: non-array tag JSON can prevent sheet preparation

Location: `scripts/utils/items.js`, `prepareEquipmentItems` tag parsing.

Valid JSON such as `null`, `"foo"` or `{}` does not enter the parse-error fallback, but the subsequent `.map` requires an array. An actor with such an imported/stored tags value can fail to render its extra sheet. A follow-up should validate the parsed shape and define a narrow, observable handling policy for unsupported values.
